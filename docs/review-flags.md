## How the bank was checked (2 Nov 2026)
Every question except scenario puzzles, syllogisms and coding was answered blind by an independent model and compared with its key; those three were proved by brute-force or model enumeration when written. Code items were compiled and run, SQL items run in sqlite3, and numeric items computed. No wrong key was found. About 140 items were reworded for clarity, second defensible answers, out-of-date facts or missing honorifics, and unit spacing was fixed in 69 more. The reworded items were not re-run through the blind check.
